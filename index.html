RuleLength × Width
The unit

One square centimetre: 1 cm²

A square: 1 cm long, 1 cm wide.

We write: cm²

We say: “square centimetre(s)”

xăng-ti-mét vuông

This shape has 6 squares.

Area = 6 cm²

Practice · Exercise 3

A piece of wood

A piece of wood is a rectangle. It is 15 cm long and 5 cm wide. What is its area?

Length: 15 cm     Width: 5 cm

Area: 15 × 5 = 75 cm²

The area of the wood is 75 cm².

Watch first

Let’s design a bedroom together

1

Place the furniture in the room. Leave space to walk!

2

Measure the bottom of each piece with a ruler.
Bed: length 12 cm, width 8 cm.

3

Calculate L × W: Bed 12 × 8 = 96 · Wardrobe 10 × 4 = 40 · Sofa 9 × 4 = 36
The plant is only decor — we don’t count it.

4

Add: 96 + 40 + 36 = 172 cm² (furniture)

5

Remaining area = 1,200 − 172 = 1,028 cm²
