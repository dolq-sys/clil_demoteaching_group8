The unit

One square centimetre: 1 cm²

A square: 1 cm long, 1 cm wide.

We write: cm²

We say: “square centimetre(s)”

Your room

What is the area of the floor?

Length = 40 cm

Width = 30 cm

Tip: 4 × 3 = 12
so 40 × 30 = 1200

40 × 30 = 1,200 cm²

The floor area is 1,200 square centimetres.

Watch first

Thầy Độ designs a bedroom

1

Place the furniture in the room. Leave space to walk!

2

Measure the bottom of each piece with a ruler.
Bed: length 12 cm, width 8 cm.

3

Calculate L × W: Bed 12 × 8 = 96 · Wardrobe 10 × 4 = 40 · Sofa 9 × 4 = 36 · Plant 3 × 3 = 9

4

Add: 96 + 40 + 36 + 9 = 181 cm² (furniture)

5

Free floor = 1,200 − 181 = 1,019 cm²

Group work

Now it's your turn!

1

2

Measure the length and width of each bottom.

3

Calculate each area: Length × Width.

4

Add all areas: total furniture area.

5

Free floor = 1,200 cm² − total.
