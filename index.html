Lesson objectives · CLIL 4Cs

By the end of the lesson, students will be able to:

Content

calculate the area of a rectangle using length × width, write it in cm², and find the remaining area by subtraction.

Communication

use the words length, width, area, square centimetres and sentence frames to describe their dream bedroom.

Cognition

apply the formula to a real-life design task and evaluate other groups’ designs.

Culture

work together in groups, explore different bedroom styles, and give fair peer feedback.

The unit

One square centimetre: 1 cm²

A square: 1 cm long, 1 cm wide.

We write: cm²

We say: “square centimetre(s)”

xăng-ti-mét vuông

Let’s design a bedroom together

1

Place the furniture in the room. Leave space to walk!

2

Measure the bottom of each piece with a ruler.
Bed: length 18 cm, width 12 cm.

3

Calculate L × W: Bed 18 × 12 = 216 · Wardrobe 10 × 5 = 50 · Sofa 16 × 4 = 64
The plant is only decor — we don’t count it.

4

Add: 216 + 50 + 64 = 330 cm² (furniture)

5

Remaining area = 1,200 − 330 = 870 cm²
